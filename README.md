# Vampire Outbreak: Quarantine Protocol

A 2D sci-fi horror survival game.

## Story

You wake up aboard a space station filled with gas. A contagion has torn through
the crew, and you survived by mutating into something else: a **vampire**.

> "Abandon your humanity for the greater good and for the protection of humans.
> Execute your tasks, eliminate infected friends, and reach the Storage Unit to
> purge the outbreak."

## Controls

| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Move | **W A S D** or the **arrow keys** |
| Interact / use / collect | **E** |
| Neck snap (kill an infected crew member) | **Q** |
| Read the story logs | Click **LOGS** on the screen |
| See the controls again | Click the **?** button on the screen |
| Fullscreen (in the .exe) | **F11** |

## How to play

1. Click **AWAKEN ONBOARD VESSEL** to start.
2. Follow the **mission objective** shown at the top of the screen. The first one is
   to follow the pulsing green beacon to the water sink and press **E**.
3. Finish the station tasks. They include a wiring minigame, steering the starship
   from the Helm Control Room, and purging the outbreak in the Storage Vault.
4. Get close to an infected crew member and press **Q** to snap their neck.
5. Once the airlock unlocks, head through the glowing green door on the right wall.

## Watch out for

- Sudden **station blackouts**
- Emergency **gate lockdowns**
- The **Apex Stalker**, lurking in the shadows

Collect and read the **story logs** as you go. They fill in what happened on the station.
